Annot Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Applies to Annot — site to source, versions 0.1.x

Annot helps you select website elements, record requested changes, and export a prompt with context for a coding assistant. Annot processes and stores its working data in your browser. The extension does not send that data to the developer or to a server, and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or remotely hosted executable code.

Information Annot handles

  • Project information you enter: project name, checkout path, deployed and development base URLs, and optional source-file hints.
  • Annotations you save: your change request, its page or shared-component scope, the page URL and title, capture time, viewport dimensions, selected element selector and tag, selected and nearby text, selected attributes, bounds, and some computed styles.
  • Temporary session information: which tabs you opened Annot in and their origins, used to keep Annot available during same-origin navigation.

Annot inspects elements under your pointer during selection and records element context when you select an element. It does not read input values, passwords, cookies, or browser history records. Captured page text, element attributes, project paths, and URLs can nevertheless contain personal or confidential information displayed on the page, including information in query strings or fragments. Select only content you intend to include and review the prompt before sharing it.

How information is used

The extension uses this information only to show your annotations, associate them with a project, translate deployed URLs into development URLs, and compile or export the prompt you request. It does not access your checkout or send requests to your development server. The extension has access to a tab only after you invoke it and while Chrome maintains that temporary permission; it does not run a background crawler.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Project profiles and saved annotations are retained in Chrome extension local storage until you remove them or uninstall the extension. You can remove saved annotations in the workspace. Uninstalling Annot removes its stored profiles and annotations. Unsaved work is held in page memory. Tab activation records use Chrome session storage and are cleared when Annot is closed for that tab, the tab is closed, or the browser session ends.

This storage is local to your browser profile and is not an Annot cloud service. People or software with access to your browser profile may be able to access its data. Files you download and prompts you copy remain wherever you save or paste them; remove those copies separately.

Export and third parties

When you choose Copy prompt, Annot writes the compiled prompt to your clipboard if the browser permits. When you choose Download prompt, it creates a local text file. Annot does not automatically transmit the prompt to Codex, OpenAI, another AI provider, or any other third party. If you paste or upload the prompt into another service, your use of that service and its handling of the prompt are governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

The developer does not receive, sell, share, or use your annotation data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or purposes unrelated to Annot’s stated functionality. Annot’s use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • activeTab: lets Annot inspect the page you explicitly open it on.
  • scripting: injects the bundled selection overlay and workspace into that tab.
  • storage: saves project profiles, annotations, and temporary tab activation state.

Changes and contact

Future releases may update this policy to reflect changes in functionality or data handling. The effective date and applicable version appear above. For privacy questions or support, use the developer contact information provided on Annot’s Chrome Web Store listing. If you contact the developer directly, only include information you intend to share.
